# Operator guide

## At shift start

Confirm current source advice, the latest approved operating authority / BC Manual references, the validated register and the after-hours mechanism. Open Current events and check overdue reviews and Critical Operations hand-offs. Confirm whose browser contains the working copy; agree one recorder. Importing another operator's backup is an explicit handover, not synchronisation.

## During a fast event

1. Enter the authoritative advice, affected geography and rate of change.
2. Identify people, centres, commuting areas and travelling teams exposed.
3. Check actual impacts, including power / connectivity and unsafe access.
4. Review the system evidence and select a defensible human posture. Levels may be skipped.
5. Communicate authoritative safety advice without waiting for an unavailable authority. Continue BC&R Lead → BC Director → Divisional Executive / delegate using Teams and the confirmed after-hours mechanism.
6. People Leaders retain ordinary attendance / flexible-work authority. Record BC Director approval before broader protective attendance direction.
7. For Critical Operations YES / UNCERTAIN, use the current BC Manual for enterprise hand-off. Record who / which role received it and its status. Do not determine enterprise residual risk in this app.
8. Issue targeted email / People Leader cascade with what, where / when, implications, action and next update. Record final issued text.
9. Set next review, assign actions, save and export a backup after material changes.

## Handover

Export all data, record the export time and deliver through approved internal handling. Incoming operator restores into a controlled profile, confirms event/version counts, last posture and next review, and agrees that their copy is authoritative for the next shift. Retain the previous backup. Do not maintain competing live copies without a reconciliation owner.

## Recovery and close

Warning expiry is one input. Continue T4 while residual constraints remain. Confirm enterprise advice before normal attendance. Record welfare, transport, utilities, backlog, communication and lessons. Resolve or explicitly hand off residual items with supporting evidence before marking checks clear and closing. Export the closed record and whole-data backup according to internal retention requirements.

## Escalation when software is unavailable

Follow the existing plan / BC Manual and authoritative emergency channels. Record advice, exposure, posture, human authority, action, hand-off, communication and next review in an approved alternative log. Re-enter later with a note explaining actual event timing. The application is an aid, not an emergency communication system.
